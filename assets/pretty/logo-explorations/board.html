Marks for the sidebar and lockups, transparent.

2. Quiet app icons

Full-bleed studies. Sage ceramic and champagne aluminum are the closest to something you’d put in a Dock.

3. Keep the chubby Pretty T3, lose the candy

Same rounded letter as today, flattened into scenery inks. If the current shapes are the thing you like, start here rather than the official T3 path.

4. Wordmarks

For the README, marketing, and the disk-image header. T3 stays geometric; Pretty can be a quiet serif.

5. The landscape is the mark

World Scenery already is the identity. These treat the photograph as the icon, with T3 as a cartographic note.

6. Analog and material

Physical objects, for taste, not Dock icons. Useful if we want the mark to feel printed or made.
