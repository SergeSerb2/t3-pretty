# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who run coding agents for hours at a time and use their phone or iPad as a remote control surface while away from the workstation. They need to see what needs attention, move between projects and environments, read substantial technical output, respond to agents, and handle source-control work without losing context.

## Product Purpose

T3 Pretty Mobile is the portable face of a live coding workspace. It keeps the upstream T3 Code workflow, providers, remote connections, and state compatibility intact while making high-frequency supervision feel calm, legible, and personal. Success means a user can understand the state of their work at a glance, enter the right thread with confidence, and complete a meaningful intervention from a small screen without returning to desktop.

## Brand Personality

Observant, composed, and alive. T3 Pretty should feel like a finely made field instrument: precise enough for professional work, quiet enough for long sessions, and connected to a larger world through its scenery rather than decorated like a generic AI product.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic chatbot, a neon terminal dashboard, a card-heavy SaaS admin, or a decorative glassmorphism concept. Avoid ornamental gradients, constant motion, oversized status metrics, invented navigation, and visual effects that compete with transcripts, code, diffs, or controls. Do not trade remote reliability, native conventions, or rendering performance for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

1. Attention before inventory: show what is active, waiting, or failed before exposing the full archive.
2. Scenery creates place: imagery can set atmosphere and orientation, but working content always owns the foreground.
3. Native where it matters: navigation, menus, sheets, gestures, haptics, keyboard behavior, and accessibility should feel at home on iOS.
4. Progressive density: the first glance is calm; project, branch, provider, environment, and source-control detail remain one gesture away.
5. Motion tells the truth: animate state changes and spatial transitions only, and stop work when content is offscreen or Reduce Motion is enabled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls, preserve Dynamic Type scaling, VoiceOver labels and state, 44-point touch targets, keyboard and pointer access on iPad, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, and light/dark appearance. Status must never rely on color alone, long project and branch names must remain usable, and all essential actions need a non-gesture path.
