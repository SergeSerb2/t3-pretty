# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers who supervise coding agents from a phone or tablet while away from their workstation. They range from experienced maintainers to trusted family members using a connected environment, and need to understand what requires attention, move between projects, read technical output, respond to agents, and handle source-control work without desktop access.

## Product Purpose

T3 Pretty Mobile is the portable face of a live coding workspace. It preserves T3 Code providers, remote connections, T3 Connect compatibility, and state contracts while making high-frequency supervision calm, legible, and personal. Success means a user can connect safely, understand work at a glance, enter the right thread confidently, and complete a meaningful intervention from either iOS or Android.

## Brand Personality

Observant, composed, and alive. T3 Pretty should feel like a finely made field instrument: precise enough for professional work, quiet enough for long sessions, and connected to a larger world through scenery instead of decorated like a generic AI product.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic chatbot, neon terminal dashboard, card-heavy SaaS admin, or decorative glassmorphism concept. Avoid ornamental gradients, constant motion, oversized status metrics, invented navigation, and visual effects that compete with transcripts, code, diffs, or controls. Do not copy one platform's chrome onto another or trade remote reliability, native conventions, accessibility, or rendering performance for novelty.

## Design Principles

1. Attention before inventory: show what is active, waiting, or failed before exposing the full archive.
2. Scenery creates place: imagery can set atmosphere and orientation, but working content always owns the foreground.
3. Native where it matters: navigation, menus, sheets, gestures, haptics, keyboard behavior, and accessibility should feel at home on both iOS and Android.
4. Progressive density: the first glance is calm; project, branch, provider, environment, and source-control detail remain one gesture away.
5. Motion tells the truth: animate state changes and spatial transitions only, and stop work when content is offscreen or reduced motion is enabled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls; preserve Dynamic Type or Android font scaling, VoiceOver and TalkBack labels and state, 44-point or 48-dp touch targets, keyboard and pointer access on tablets, reduced motion, reduced transparency, and light/dark appearance. Status must never rely on color alone, long project and branch names must remain usable, and all essential actions need a non-gesture path.
